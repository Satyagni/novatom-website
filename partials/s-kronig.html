Kronig: device simulation from a plain-English request.

Describe the device as you would to a colleague. Kronig asks only for what is missing, proposes a plan for your approval, runs the simulation, and reports a result only after independent checks on the solver output have passed. In our platform it is the device layer: it predicts how a device built from a discovered material behaves.

Desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Runs on DEVSIM locally or on Synopsys Sentaurus over SSH.

Validation is code, not judgement.

A run is reported as passed only when independent checks on the solver output agree. Twelve of the more than twenty checks are listed.

  • Solver convergence at every point
  • Poisson residual consistency
  • Finite values everywhere
  • Band ordering
  • Mesh halving re-solve, 3% limit
  • Carrier statistics applicability
  • Grid convergence index (Roache)
  • Quasi-static versus AC capacitance
  • Terminal current conservation
  • Sweep continuity
  • Requested outputs present
  • Study order preserved
